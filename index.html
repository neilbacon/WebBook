CHAPTER I

The Barrington Wilderness

Rising high above the Hunter Valley in New South Wales, Australia, Barrington Tops National Park is a sanctuary of breathtaking diversity. It forms a crucial link in the World Heritage-listed Gondwana Rainforests, protecting ecological systems that have persisted for millions of years.

This photographic journey explores the park's contrasting landscapes, moving from the mist-shrouded wetlands of Polblue Swamp at 1,500 meters altitude, through ancient temperate rainforests of giant Antarctic Beech, and outward to the majestic volcanic lookouts.

Each page captures a moment in this alpine wilderness, documenting a fragile yet resilient ecosystem where moisture, altitude, and isolation shape a unique botanical world.
